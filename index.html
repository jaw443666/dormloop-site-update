FAQ

Questions students and parents ask.

Who can buy and sell on DormLoop?

Only verified University of Miami students, our pilot campus. You sign up with your miami.edu email, which keeps the marketplace closed to campus and safer than open listing sites. Parents can reserve a move in bundle here, or pay through a link their student shares from the app.

What is a Loop Day?

It is the day our trucks visit your residential area during move out. You book a one hour slot in the app, and a crew of four comes to your room with carts. Each item gets a tracking tag and a condition photo at your door, then the crew carries it to the truck.

What does Store It include?

One price of $49 per item covers anything one person can carry for the whole summer: pickup from your room, a tracking tag and condition photo, climate controlled storage through hurricane season, delivery to your fall room on move in day, and a free kit of boxes and tape. You can add your fall room later, once housing assignments come out.

What if I'm leaving to study abroad or I'm an international student?

Sell It is built for this. We pick up on Loop Day and sell your items to an incoming student while you're away, so nothing gets shipped overseas. If you're back on campus in the fall, Store It holds your things for the summer.

How do you make sure items are in good shape?

Every DormLoop Certified item is cleaned, checked and photographed by our crew before it goes into storage. Student listings show the seller's rating, and we hold your payment until you confirm you have the item.

What happens to items that don't sell?

Sell It items that haven't sold by move in are donated to a local charity partner, and the seller gets the donation receipt. That's also why Sell It works best for items worth $25 or more. Smaller things are free to list yourself.

What if my pickup or delivery goes wrong?

Every item is tagged and photographed at your door, so there is always a record of what we took and the condition it was in. You can follow each item in the app from pickup to delivery. If something goes wrong, email us and we'll make it right.
